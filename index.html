Questions

What is SunsetPing?

SunsetPing scores tonight's sunset from 1 to 10 for a city or ZIP code and can email you the same day. You can check a place with no account.

How does the sunset score work?

The score looks at low, mid, and high cloud around sunset, plus humidity, visibility, and rain. Enough cloud to catch color scores higher. A gray blanket or a completely clear sky scores lower. 8 or above is Don't miss it. 6 is worth stepping outside. 4 is a quick peek.

What is free versus Pro?

Free is one location and a same-day sunset alert. Pro is up to 10 saved spots, sunrise alerts, a 7-day outlook, a day-before heads-up at 4:00 PM local, a score threshold, and a best-spot ranking.

How much does Pro cost?

Pro is $3.99 a month or $29 a year. Yearly saves 39% compared with paying monthly. The longer answers live on the FAQ, and city pages start at sunset cities.
