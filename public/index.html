Questions

What is SunsetPing?

SunsetPing tells landscape photographers whether the light is worth the drive. You type a city or ZIP code and get a score from 1 to 10, the local sunset time, golden hour, and one sentence on why. Checking a score does not require an account. The email alerts are free.

How does the sunset score work?

The score looks at low, mid, and high cloud around sunset, plus humidity, visibility, and rain. Enough cloud to catch color scores higher. A gray blanket or a completely clear sky scores lower. 8 or above is Don't miss it. 6 is worth stepping outside. 4 is a quick peek.

What do free alerts include?

Everything. Up to 10 saved spots, sunrise alerts, a 7-day outlook, a day-before heads-up at 4:00 PM local, a score threshold, and a best-spot ranking. The price is 0.

How much does SunsetPing cost?

SunsetPing is free. Sign up with your email and a city. There is no paid plan. The longer answers live on the FAQ, and city pages start at sunset cities.
